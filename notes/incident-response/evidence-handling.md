# Digital Evidence Handling

## Goals
Evidence handling aims to preserve integrity, provenance, and traceability so findings can be reviewed and reproduced.

## Basic process
1. Follow the organization's incident response and legal procedures.
2. Record who collected the evidence, when, from which system, and why.
3. Preserve original data and work from copies when feasible.
4. Calculate cryptographic hashes for files or forensic images when appropriate.
5. Store evidence in access-controlled locations.
6. Record every transfer, access, and transformation.
7. Keep a clear distinction between observations, interpretations, and hypotheses.

## Chain of custody
A chain-of-custody record documents possession and handling over time. Missing details can weaken confidence in the evidence.

## Important caution
Do not power off, reboot, or modify a potentially compromised system without considering volatility and the approved response plan.

## Key takeaway
Good analysis depends on evidence that is preserved and documented carefully.
