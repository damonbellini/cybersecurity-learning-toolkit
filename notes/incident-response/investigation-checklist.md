# Incident Investigation Checklist

A basic investigation can follow this sequence:

1. Define the alert and scope.
2. Preserve relevant evidence.
3. Establish a timeline.
4. Identify affected accounts and systems.
5. Look for related indicators.
6. Contain confirmed threats according to the response plan.
7. Document findings and actions.
8. Record lessons learned.

Do not destroy evidence during investigation unless the response plan explicitly requires it.
