# Human Oversight for Agents

## When to involve a human
- Evidence is missing or contradictory.
- A proposed action has significant impact.
- The request is outside the agent's defined scope.
- Confidence is low or a required check failed.

## Approval design
Show the proposed action, supporting evidence, expected impact, and rollback considerations before requesting approval.

## Review question
Can the human make an informed decision without repeating the entire investigation?
