# Evidence, Confidence, and Uncertainty

## Evidence first
Recommendations should be traceable to concrete observations, not just a confidence score or fluent explanation.

## Good output habits
- Cite the log, event, or artifact supporting each finding.
- Separate observed facts from inferences.
- Identify missing and contradictory evidence.
- Use an explicit unknown or undetermined value when evidence is insufficient.

## Review question
Could another analyst reproduce the conclusion from the cited evidence?
