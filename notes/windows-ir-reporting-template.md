# Windows Incident Response Reporting Template

## 1. Executive summary

Describe the incident, known business impact, current status and immediate decisions needed.

## 2. Scope

List systems, identities, business units, time window and evidence sources included. Explicitly identify exclusions.

## 3. Key findings

For each finding, include:
- Statement
- Supporting evidence and source reference
- Timestamp and affected asset
- Confidence level
- Alternative explanations or limitations

## 4. Timeline

Include the key events with time zone normalization and links to original evidence.

## 5. Containment and remediation

Record actions taken, approvals, action owners, timestamps and observed results.

## 6. Gaps and uncertainty

List unavailable logs, retention gaps, unexamined systems and hypotheses that remain unconfirmed.

## 7. Recommendations

Prioritize actions by risk, urgency, dependencies and business impact. Separate immediate containment from longer-term hardening.

## 8. Appendix

Include collection methods, tool versions, query details, hashes and chain-of-custody records. Avoid including unnecessary secrets or personal data.
