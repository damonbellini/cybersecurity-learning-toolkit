# Cryptography Fundamentals

Cryptography protects information using mathematical techniques.

## Main Goals

Confidentiality protects data from unauthorised disclosure.

Integrity helps detect unauthorised modification.

Authenticity helps establish who or what is communicating.

Non repudiation can provide evidence that an action or message originated from a particular key holder.

Study the difference between encoding, encryption, hashing and digital signatures before moving into advanced cryptography.
