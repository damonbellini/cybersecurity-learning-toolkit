# Digital Forensics Fundamentals

Digital forensics is the structured examination of digital evidence to understand events and support investigations.

## Core Concepts

Preserve evidence before analysis. Maintain a clear chain of custody. Work from copies where possible. Record tools, commands, timestamps and findings.

## Evidence Sources

Disk images
Memory captures
Logs
Browser artefacts
Files and metadata
Network captures

Forensic conclusions should be supported by reproducible evidence rather than assumptions.
