# Incident Response Workflow

## 1. Detect

Identify an alert, suspicious event or reported security issue.

## 2. Investigate

Collect evidence, establish scope and build a timeline.

## 3. Classify

Determine severity, affected assets and likely impact.

## 4. Contain

Limit further damage while preserving useful evidence.

## 5. Eradicate

Remove the root cause, malicious artefacts or compromised access.

## 6. Recover

Restore normal operations and monitor for recurrence.

## 7. Learn

Document findings and improve controls, detections and procedures.
