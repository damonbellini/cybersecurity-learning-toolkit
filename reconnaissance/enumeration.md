# Enumeration

Enumeration is the structured process of identifying services, versions, users, shares, directories and other exposed information after initial discovery.

## Workflow

1. Identify reachable hosts.
2. Identify open ports.
3. Identify services and versions.
4. Research the exposed technology.
5. Record findings and evidence.
6. Validate weaknesses only within authorised scope.

Useful tools include Nmap, curl, browser developer tools and service specific enumeration utilities.
