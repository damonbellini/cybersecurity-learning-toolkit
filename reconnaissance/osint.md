# OSINT

Open Source Intelligence is the collection and analysis of information from publicly available sources.

## Core Concepts

Identify the objective, define scope, collect information, verify sources, correlate findings and document evidence.

## Useful Areas

Domain and DNS information
Public code repositories
Public documents and metadata
Certificate transparency
Search engine research
Public breach notifications and security reports

## Good Practice

Separate facts from assumptions. Record the source and date of important findings. Do not attempt to access private accounts or systems without authorisation.
