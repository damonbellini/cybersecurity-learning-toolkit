# SOC Tiers

## Tier 1 — Monitoring and Triage

Typical responsibilities:

- Monitor dashboards
- Review alerts
- Validate suspicious activity
- Enrich alerts with context
- Escalate confirmed or complex incidents

## Tier 2 — Investigation

Typical responsibilities:

- Investigate endpoints and accounts
- Correlate multiple log sources
- Determine attack scope
- Identify indicators of compromise
- Recommend containment actions

## Tier 3 — Advanced Security

Typical responsibilities:

- Threat hunting
- Detection engineering
- Malware analysis
- Advanced incident response
- Adversary research

## Additional roles

- SOC manager
- Incident responder
- Detection engineer
- Threat hunter
- Digital forensics analyst
- Threat intelligence analyst
